\section{Scope and Reproduction}
This supplement retains measurements and controls that would otherwise obscure the main question. It does not introduce new training, expand the eligible cohort, or select additional calibration weights. All machine-readable sources are listed with SHA-256 digests in the accompanying evidence manifest. Figure generation reads saved JSON artifacts, not manually transcribed Markdown tables. The source repository's large experiment outputs remain local; small aggregate snapshots used by the manuscript are included for audit.

The primary cohort is six domain--activity classes, five contents per class, four repetitions, and two deployments. The 240 visits yield 30 independent content groups for resampling. Five content folds give 192/48 train/test visits for jointly seen deployments, or 96 source training visits, 24 source holdout visits, and 24 target holdout visits for source-only fitting. The target deployment's 96 other visits are excluded from numerical fitting. There is no additional validation set or test-driven early stopping in these fixed logistic/affine experiments.

\section{A Short Derivation of the Center Identity}
Use common coordinates with fixed positive-semidefinite metric $M$. Expanding $\|q_i-\mu_g-\epsilon_i\|_M^2-\|q_i-\mu_g\|_M^2$ gives $\epsilon_i^{\mathsf T}M\epsilon_i-2(q_i-\mu_g)^{\mathsf T}M\epsilon_i$. Since $\sum_{i\in g}\epsilon_i=0$, replacing $q_i-\mu_g$ in the cross-term by $q_i-\bar q_g$ does not change the group sum. Summing over groups yields the main-text identity. The same regularizer cancels. Incomplete targets, unequal observation weights, or coordinate-specific masks require matching weighted centers; the unweighted identity should not be applied indiscriminately.

For a fixed input and averaging over every target in a group, the residual cross-term vanishes and the difference from a center loss is constant in the prediction. A cyclic donor retains a particular input--residual association and generally does not have this equivalence. Neither result says that the residual is causally irrelevant to labels.
